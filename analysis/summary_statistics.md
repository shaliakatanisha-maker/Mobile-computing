## Key Analytical Findings

1. **Time-of-Day Congestion Impact**:
   - At **P2 (Goda Uda Canteen)**, lunchtime crowd increases latency on campus Wi-Fi significantly (e.g. from ~42 ms in morning to ~60+ ms at lunchtime on Huawei GR5, and from ~27 ms to ~39 ms on Xiaomi Pad 6).
   - Download throughput at P2 drops correspondingly due to 802.11 channel contention and airtime contention.

2. **Wi-Fi vs Cellular on Huawei GR5**:
   - **Outdoors (P1 Central Court)**: Mobile data outperforms Wi-Fi (approx. 24–28 Mbps vs 6–8 Mbps) because Wi-Fi signals attenuate over open distance from building access points, while 4G LTE has clear line-of-sight.
   - **Indoors (P4 Corridor & P5 Lab)**: Wi-Fi outperforms Cellular. In P4, concrete walls attenuate cellular signal down to ~ -110 dBm, dropping cellular throughput to ~5 Mbps, whereas campus Wi-Fi retains 13–15 Mbps.

3. **Device & Wi-Fi Standard Comparison (Huawei GR5 vs Xiaomi Pad 6)**:
   - The **Xiaomi Pad 6 (Wi-Fi 6, 5 GHz)** consistently outperforms the **Huawei GR5 (Wi-Fi 4, 2.4 GHz)** across all locations, achieving 2x to 3x higher download speeds (e.g., ~60 Mbps vs ~26 Mbps in P5 Lab) and lower RTT latency, reflecting advancements in modern radio chipsets, OFDMA, and MIMO antenna design.
